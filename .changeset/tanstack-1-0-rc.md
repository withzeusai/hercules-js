First 1.0 release candidate for the TanStack Start packages. `@usehercules/vite-tanstack` gives a Hercules TanStack Start app its whole Vite and Vitest setup from `defineConfig()` and `defineVitestConfig()`, and `@usehercules/auth-tanstack` sends failed sign-in callbacks back into the app with `?auth_error=<reason>`. Both are published under the `rc` dist-tag while the Hercules TanStack Start template adopts them.
